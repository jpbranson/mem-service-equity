Status: **draft, not sent.** Use it only if TDH has not responded by the end
of **Friday 2026-10-02**. The request went to TDH on Wednesday 2026-09-23,

in between).

## What TDH owes by then

Under T.C.A. § 10-7-503(a)(2)(B), a records custodian who cannot produce the
records promptly must respond within seven business days in one of three
ways:
1. make the records available;
2. deny the request in writing, with the legal basis; or
3. send a completed records request response form with the time reasonably
   needed to produce them.

## Which version to send

- **Nothing received by 2026-10-02:** send the letter below on Monday
  2026-10-05.
- **Response form with an estimated date:** don't send anything. Record the
  estimated date and any reference number in DECISIONS.md H11, and follow up
  if that date passes.
- **Told the records belong to the county:** send the original letter to the
  Shelby County Health Department (details in `h11-food-inspections.md`),
  and note this in DECISIONS.md H11.
- **Denied in whole or part:** record the stated legal basis in DECISIONS.md
  H11 and decide whether to narrow the request or contest the denial.

If a follow-up also goes unanswered, the Comptroller's Office of Open
Records Counsel handles questions from requesters about access to state
records. A court petition under T.C.A. § 10-7-505 is the formal remedy. That
decision is for the requester, not the project.
