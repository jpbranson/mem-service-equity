**Re: Public records request for Shelby County food establishment inspection data**

To the Public Records Request Coordinator:

Under the Tennessee Public Records Act, T.C.A. § 10-7-503, I request copies of
the records described below. I am a Tennessee citizen. A copy of my
government-issued identification is attached.

Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1200-23-01-.08(4)(c)5 says the department shall treat
food establishment inspection reports as public documents. Individual reports
are already available one at a time on the department's inspection portal. I
am asking for the same information in bulk.

### Records requested

For all food service establishments in Shelby County, for inspections
conducted from **January 1, 2021 through the date this request is
processed**:

1. **Inspections.** One record per inspection, with:
   - permit number, or other establishment identifier
   - establishment name
   - establishment street address, city and ZIP code
   - inspection date
   - inspection type or purpose (for example routine, follow-up, complaint,
     pre-opening)
   - inspection score
2. **Violations.** One record per violation cited, with:
   - the identifier linking it to the inspection above
   - violation item number or code
   - violation description
   - whether it was a priority, priority foundation or core item, or the
     point value, whichever is recorded
   - whether it was corrected during the inspection, if recorded
3. **Establishments.** A list of food service establishments permitted in
   Shelby County at any time during the same period, with permit number,
   name, address, establishment type or risk category if recorded, and permit
   issue, expiration or closure dates if recorded. This includes
   establishments with no inspections in the period.
4. **Code list.** Any existing data dictionary, code list or field
   definitions for the records above.

### Format

I request these records in electronic, machine-readable form, such as CSV
files, Excel workbooks or a database export, if the data is kept
electronically. I am **not** asking for printed or PDF copies of individual
inspection reports, and I would prefer not to receive them in place of an
export.

If any field is not kept, please leave it out and produce the rest. If any
part of the request is denied, please produce the remaining records and state
the legal basis for each denial in writing.

If producing the full period is a problem, please produce the most recent 24
months first and the earlier records afterward.

### Fees

If fees will exceed $[amount], please send me an itemized estimate before
fulfilling the request. I would prefer electronic delivery by email or file
transfer, which I understand should reduce copying costs.

### Recurring updates

Separately from this request, I would like to know whether the department, or
its inspection-system vendor, can provide the same export on a recurring
basis, such as monthly, or offers a data feed. I understand the Act covers
records that exist at the time of a request. This is a question, not part of
the request.

### Purpose (optional)

These records will be used for Memphis Service Equity, a non-commercial
public project that reports how consistently public services are delivered
across Memphis neighborhoods. The data will be summarized by area and
published with its methods.

I understand the Act requires a response within seven business days: the
records, a denial with its legal basis, or an explanation of the time needed
to produce them. Please contact me with any questions about scope. I am glad
to narrow or clarify the request.
