Memphis Service Equity

Does Memphis work the same for everyone?

Five systems make promises to every Memphis address: the bus, the power, 311, restaurant inspections and neighborhood investment. This site measures whether each promise is kept, and whether the answer depends on where you live.

Look up an address

Enter a Memphis street address. The area around it means the address's hexagon on a 174-meter grid plus the six hexagons touching it, roughly a 0.3-mile radius.

Your address is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (or OpenStreetMap's Nominatim if the Census Bureau finds no match) to get its location. This site keeps nothing and has no server.
